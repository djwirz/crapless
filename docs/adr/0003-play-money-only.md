# 3. Play money only

Date: 2026-11-26
Status: Accepted

## Context
Gambling law generally turns on prize, chance and payment. Free chips with nothing for sale and nothing to win leave only chance. In Kater v. Churchill Downs (9th Circuit, 2018), virtual chips in a game that sold them were treated as a thing of value under Washington law. App stores rate simulated gambling as adult content. This is not legal advice.

## Options considered
1. **Free chips, nothing sold, nothing won.**
2. **Sell chips or extras:** brings payment and legal review into a hobby game.
3. **Prizes or sweepstakes mechanics:** treated as real gambling in a growing number of places.

## Decision
Chips are free, refill without limit, and can't be bought, sold, transferred out or redeemed. No prizes, no ads, no links to real-money gambling.

## Why
It keeps the game a game.

## Consequences
- No revenue. Any plan to charge for anything needs legal advice and a new ADR first.
- Before opening to strangers: say plainly that no money is involved, add a privacy note, decide on an age notice and re-check the law.
