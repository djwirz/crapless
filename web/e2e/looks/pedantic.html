== the rules

How to play

Crapless craps with play money. One player throws the dice; everyone can bet.

A turn

  1. Come-out. The shooter bets PASS and throws. A 7 wins. Any other total becomes the point. In crapless, 2, 3, 11 and 12 do not lose: they are points too.
  2. Keep throwing. The point again wins Pass, and the next throw is a new come-out.
  3. A 7 first is a seven-out: Pass loses and the dice move to the next player.

Bets

Pass (pays 1 to 1)
Made on the come-out. Once there is a point it stays until decided.
Odds
Extra chips behind a Pass or Come bet that has a number, up to 3 times that bet. They pay true odds, with no house edge.
Come (pays 1 to 1)
Like a new Pass bet, made while there is a point. A 7 on the next throw wins; otherwise it moves to the number thrown and wins if that comes again before a 7. Tap its chip, in the top half of that number, to add odds. Come odds are off on a come-out and are handed back if the bet is decided then.
Place
Tap a number. It pays each time that number comes before a 7, and stays up. A 7 loses it. Place bets are off on a come-out unless you switch them on.

What they pay

Number Odds Place Place in
2 or 12 6 to 1 11 to 2 2s
3 or 11 3 to 1 11 to 4 4s
4 or 10 2 to 1 9 to 5 5s
5 or 9 3 to 2 7 to 5 5s
6 or 8 6 to 5 7 to 6 6s

Pass, Come and Place bets run from 5 to 500. Chips are whole, so some bets go in multiples that pay out exactly. Odds on 5 or 9 go in 2s, and on 6 or 8 in 5s.

Pedantic mode

Switch it on from the top button. Place bets on 2, 3, 11 and 12 can then start at 4. Pick the 1 chip to bet that little. Pays do not change, and everyone is told when you switch it on or off. A small bet stays up after you switch it off, but changing it follows the usual minimums.

This table

  • Pick a chip, then tap where to bet. Tap − first to take chips off.
  • The shooter taps ROLL or flicks up on the dice.
  • The top button shows who is shooting: invite friends, pass the dice, leave.
  • Refills are free. Nothing is sold and nothing can be won.
  • A shooter away for 30 seconds loses the dice. A seat is kept for 10 minutes, longer while a bet is locked.
